Story, original pitch and adaptation

Three households open their gates in sequence to pass a trickle through terracotta channels. Water reaches the dry birdbath on the lowest balcony. A bird drinks, then flies back uphill past the opened gates.

  • 0–4 s: trickle reaches first gate
  • 4–9 s: three gates pass water downhill
  • 9–11 s: lowest birdbath fills and bird drinks
  • 11–14 s: bird returns uphill above the roofs

The original concept

A hillside block receives one trickle of water. Each household can keep it or tilt a tile toward the next balcony down. The camera follows the stream all afternoon. On the last balcony a child fills a birdbath, and the bird flies back uphill.

A downhill ribbon connects miniature architecture into a collective action, ending with the bird carrying the movement uphill.
