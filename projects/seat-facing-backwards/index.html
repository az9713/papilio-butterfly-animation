Story, original pitch and adaptation

A hand-cranked panorama box carries a painted railway past its window. At a station, the painted view runs out. A stitched edge passes and bare linen replaces the landscape. A solitary wooden viewer waits beside the empty window.

  • 0–4 s: miniature painted railway moves

  • 12–14 s: crank and viewer stop

The original concept

A widow cranks a hand-painted train journey past the window of a panorama box. Her late husband painted only the view from his seat. At their station she lets the crank run on, hoping to see herself waiting. The last handspan is bare linen.

A finite painted panorama can unfold into an emotionally striking absence, using warm mechanical intimacy instead of spectacle.
